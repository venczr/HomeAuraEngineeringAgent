6. BLOCKER — SILENT CATALOG STRING MUTATION

The independent input used visible leading/trailing spaces, repeated internal
spaces, Unicode, a full-width hyphen, a normal hyphen, and an article with an
intentional internal space:

manufacturer before: "  Acme   GmbH  "
manufacturer after:  "Acme GmbH"

brand before: "  Bränd   Pro  "
brand after:  "Bränd Pro"

model before: " M－42  Plus "
model after:  "M-42 Plus"

article before: " AB-12   34 "
article after:  "AB-12 34"

category before: " Heat   Pump "
category after:  "Heat Pump"

The same five changes occurred through:
- direct EquipmentCatalogItem constructor;
- EquipmentCatalogItem.model_validate;
- EquipmentCatalogItem.model_validate_json.

JSON round-trip after the first mutation is stable, but that does not preserve
the original user-provided identifiers. Of particular concern, NFKC changes
the full-width model hyphen to ASCII and repeated whitespace in an article is
collapsed. These transformations may change an external catalog key.

Exact root cause:
- agent/project_models.py:156-163 returns normalized display text;
- agent/project_models.py:840-853 installs that transformation as a
  before-validator on all five persisted fields.

The PROJECT-1F contract authorized:
- trim/NFKC/whitespace/casefold for computed manufacturer identity;
- a trim-based nonblank check for VERIFIED strings.

It did not establish a migration or storage contract that rewrites persisted
brand, model, article, and category identifiers. Updating the new
documentation to say "String values are normalized" does not retroactively
approve such a persistence change.

This is a commit blocker. No correction was made because PROJECT-1G is
read-only.
