# HomeAura UFH physical-layout diagnosis and correction

## Root cause

The original room coverage route was an ordered polyline, but the building
artifact generator did not preserve that physical model when it assembled the
floor plan. `scripts/build_real_floor_ufh_artifacts.py` generated each room
route separately and then fabricated supply and return transit polylines from
the manifold and riser to each room endpoint. The source opening graph was
explicitly unverified, yet those preview lanes were rendered as if they were
installed pipe.

The baseline evidence was captured in
`dev/ufh_diagnostics/baseline_20260919/`. Independent analysis of that
baseline found 67 of 72 transit polylines intersecting another room and 872
pairs of transit/coverage paths with measurable overlap or crossing. The
coverage routes themselves were separate room polylines; they were not one
floor-wide pipe, but the fabricated transit layer made them look connected
through walls and neighbouring rooms.

The original `non_crossing_visual` path intentionally preferred a compact
scanline sweep. Its points also contained repeated connector vertices, and
the old physical split could cut a route at arbitrary points without a
collector-accessible path. The legacy bifilar candidate was one continuous
polyline, but the independent gate rejected it because it had no measured
central hairpin and no interleaved return.

The coordinate audit did not find a second application of the room-to-global
transform in `globalize`; the conversion is a millimetre translation based on
the source origin and scale. The audit did find boundary-terminal spacing
adjustments created by source dimensions and arclength partitioning. These are
now reported explicitly by the physical validator instead of being hidden in
the drawing.

## Changes

- `agent/ufh_layout_engine.py` now constructs a true rectangular bifilar
  spiral from nested even-offset inward rings, an explicit centre hairpin and
  odd-offset outward rings. The result is one ordered centreline. The same
  module now removes duplicate meander vertices and avoids a terminal strip
  shorter than the minimum bend leg.
- `agent/ufh_physical_validation.py` adds generator-independent checks for
  geometry, endpoints, branches, self-intersections, obstacles, orthogonality,
  bend legs, spacing, maximum length and exact visual centreline equality.
  Hydraulic status remains `NOT_EVALUATED` until authoritative pipe, flow and
  manifold inputs exist.
- `scripts/build_real_floor_ufh_artifacts.py` promotes a spiral only after its
  independent topology gate passes. Other rooms retain a continuous meander
  or are left unresolved. Every emitted physical route receives the separate
  validation report.
- Building transit is now behind a strict display gate. Because the current
  source does not verify openings, corridor paths or riser penetrations, those
  long artificial lines are omitted from the SVG. The artifact retains the
  preview accounting and marks the two endpoints as unresolved instead of
  presenting a false physical connection.
- Red/blue flow metadata remains tied to the calculated centreline: supply is
  `#d92d20`, return is `#1570ef`, with `data-flow-role` on every rendered
  segment.

The current Test_01 plan has 16 room records, 15 routed rooms and 36 physical
coverage circuits. The independent physical validator passes 36/36 emitted
coverage polylines. One room currently uses the promoted true spiral; the
remaining regular or irregular rooms use a validated continuous meander, and
room 9 remains geometry-unresolved.

## Remaining engineering limits

- Building-level manifold transit is unresolved until the source or installer
  confirms every door/opening, corridor lane and riser penetration.
- Room 2 and room 9 still require exact stair boundary and passage continuity
  evidence.
- Hydraulic validation, balancing, pressure loss, flow, pump and mixing-unit
  selection are not performed because authoritative engineering inputs are
  absent.
- The output remains a geometry-only preview and is not a construction issue
  package. The 90 m circuit policy is a preview gate until project-owned pipe
  and hydraulic parameters are bound.
