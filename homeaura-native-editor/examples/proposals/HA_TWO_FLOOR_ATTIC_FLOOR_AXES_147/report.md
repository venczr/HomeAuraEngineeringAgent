# D147 - attic floor axes

Eleven continuous handoff-to-body-to-handoff floor axes are published. A-C05 is retired; its territory is served by the enlarged single hall circuit A-C06 and the 22-leg heated transit fanout. Every floor axis is within 40-80 m, has no self/inter-route contact and avoids the stair void. A 1.4 m allowance is reserved for the next accessible K2 service-bridge block; no hidden screed joint is permitted. Exact polygon coverage is still diagnostic.
